Questions to ask before choosing a program

The service you need may change as your child gets older. Day care, preschool, after-school care, and summer camp each serve a different purpose, so avoid assuming that one option automatically covers another. Confirm the details for the specific program you’re considering.

Keep these points handy when you contact the school:
- Your child’s age and the dates you’re considering.
- The days and hours you need care.
- Whether you’re asking about preschool, after-school care, or summer camp.
- Questions about the daily routine and family communication.
- What paperwork or items to prepare before your child begins.

One common oversight is waiting to ask about schedule and enrollment details until care is needed right away. Another is choosing based on a program name without learning what it includes. Ask the academy to clarify anything you’re unsure about, and check current details directly rather than relying on a general description.
