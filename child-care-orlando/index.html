Match care to your child’s stage

Child care can mean different things at different points in childhood. A family may need care for an infant, an education-focused preschool program, help covering the hours after school, or a plan for summer. Knowing which of those needs comes first will make conversations with providers more useful. For Child Care Orlando families, a good starting point is to describe your child’s current age and the routine you want help with, then ask what program options are available.

A range of programs at Ladybird Academy

Ladybird Academy of Dr Phillips offers early childhood care and education for children from 6 weeks through 12 years old. Its services include day care, preschool, an after-school program, and summer camp. The academy’s information describes flexible, affordable programs in a state of the art award winning facility. Those details can help you decide what to ask about, but your own schedule and your child’s needs should guide the discussion. You can call to clarify which program fits the age and care arrangement you have in mind.

A practical checklist for your call

Before contacting a provider, write down a few points so you don’t have to remember them on the spot:

  • Your child’s age and the program you’re interested in
  • The days and hours you need care
  • Whether you’re asking about preschool, after-school care, or summer
  • Any questions about what to prepare before enrollment

Ladybird Academy of Dr Phillips lists weekday hours of 6:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. with the academy closed Saturday and Sunday. If your plans depend on a particular schedule or program, confirm those details by phone.

Looking for child care around Orlando

Orlando parents can approach the search by comparing the care each provider offers with the stage their child is in. At Ladybird Academy of Dr Phillips, the stated age range extends from early infancy to 12 years, with programs that include both preschool and school-age options. That range gives you a reason to ask about more than one type of care if your family’s needs change during the year. Child Care Orlando searches can turn up many kinds of services, so be clear about whether you need daytime care, a preschool program, after-school support, or summer care.

Talk through your options

Avoid assuming that a program name tells you everything about the hours, age fit, or arrangements. Ask direct questions and make sure the answers match what your family needs. If you’re unsure which service to ask about, explain your child’s age and describe a typical week. The academy can then discuss its programs and the details you should consider.
